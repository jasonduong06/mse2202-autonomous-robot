## Final Selection

The **Three-Part Scavenger, Elevator, and Sorting Robots** (Concept 4) was selected as the final concept and built upon over time.

### Interpretation of Matrix
- **Weight Distribution:** With each subsystem only handing one task each part will be simple and relatively light weight compared to a large heavy all in one system. 
- **Ease of Implementation:** Breaking the system into three simple subsystems allows for easier devlopment, and simpilar troubleshooting for each part compared to complex mechanisms
- **Reliability:** Simple sub systems have fewer moving parts reduces failure risk and increases robustness
- **Ease of Repair:** Similar to implimentation with each subsystem only serving one function troubleshotting and repair is much easier than determening a failure mode of a large complex mechanism
- **Scalability & Modularity:** Having three seperate systems there is a lot of room to make adjustments and add more features to each

### Summary

The Three-Part Robot concept was chosen because it offered a practical compromise between performance and implementation difficulty. Dispite it introducing more subsystems than the other concepts, it is able to reduced the functional difficulty placed on any one subsystem and allows the design to better address object collection, transfer, and sorting as separate engineering problems. After comparing it to the other possible concepts against our determined constraints  this concept was identified as the most promising design for refinement and detailed development.
